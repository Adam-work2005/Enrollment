About Garcia College of Technology

Established in 2025, Garcia College of Technology (GCT) is dedicated to promoting academic excellence and providing a transformative educational experience. Our mission is to equip students with the skills and knowledge necessary to thrive in the 21st century.

Our Mission

To provide affordable and accessible quality education that nurtures innovation, creativity, and critical thinking, empowering our students to become leaders in their respective fields.

Our Vision

To be a premier educational institution recognized for excellence in teaching and learning, fostering a diverse and inclusive community that contributes positively to society.

Core Values

  • Integrity: We uphold the highest standards of ethics and accountability in all our endeavors.
  • Innovation: We encourage creativity and embrace new ideas to enhance the learning experience.
  • Inclusivity: We value diversity and promote an environment where everyone feels valued and respected.
  • Excellence: We strive for the highest quality in education and support services, continuously seeking improvement.

Our Commitment

At GCT, we are committed to student success and engagement. Our experienced faculty provides personalized attention, and hands-on learning opportunities equip students with the practical skills needed to excel in their fields. We believe in nurturing not only academic achievement but also character development, preparing our students for successful careers and responsible citizenship.
