Our Courses

At Garcia College of Technology, we offer a variety of programs designed to prepare you for success in your chosen field. Explore our courses below to find the right fit for your academic and career goals.

BSCE

Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering

This program focuses on the design, construction, and maintenance of infrastructure such as bridges, roads, and buildings.

BSEE

Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering

This course covers the fundamentals of electrical circuits, systems, and electronic devices, preparing you for a career in various sectors like power generation, telecommunications, and more.

BSME

Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering

Students will learn about mechanics, thermodynamics, and materials science to design and manufacture mechanical systems and devices.

BSIT

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

This program prepares students for IT careers, covering topics like programming, database management, and network administration.

BSCE

Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering

Focus on the integration of hardware and software in computing systems, enabling students to develop innovative tech solutions.
